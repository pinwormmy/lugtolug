# Fratello expansion import

Recovered from the unmerged `codex/fratello-lug-data` branch (commit 6816f82, 2026-06-29). Its 79 rows were matched against the current seed by hand instead of merging the stale branch.

- 44 new watches (ids 8167–8210); brand spellings follow the existing seed rows.
- 34 rows describe watches already in the seed; only the Fratello source was appended to the existing row, metrics were left unchanged so display groups stay intact.
- 1 dropped: Nomos Club Campus All Olive and Full Rose (Fratello gives 44.3 mm lug-to-lug against 46.7 mm on the NOMOS Club Campus 38 rows).
